# UW-IPL — EURS Track1, submission 19050 (v4 Boundary)

Restoration uses label-conditioned E034 Restormer plus a bounded physics data-consistency correction. Only trailing water-type/depth labels are read. Segmentation combines frozen SAM3 predictions on original RGB, a smaller Restormer384 reconstruction and the E034/physics reconstruction. TRAIN-calibrated class-selective score fusion and a TRAIN-only ConceptBank combine these predictions. Frozen CascadePSP refines OTHER boundaries using original and Restormer384 views; conservative seed-connected additions and background-only removals protect other classes.

E034 trained on the 14 scenes and validated on the six scenes listed in FACTSHEET.json. It inherited SIDD-pretrained Restormer and used UIIS10K/USIS10K clean-source degradation synthesis. SAM3/ConceptBank calibration used 320 TRAIN images from 16 scenes. Local segmentation selection reused fish12, fish21, ray1 and seahorse1. The latter two were in E034 training, so local restoration-assisted results are not independent generalization estimates. Testing images did not train or tune this final lineage. DATA_AND_LICENSES.md lists external assets and known or unresolved licenses.

Inference is image-independent, fixed-seed and eval-only. Images are decoded individually; aspect ratio is preserved and output longest side is at most 1024. Original/physics SAM3 branches use 1008-square letterboxing; the auxiliary restorer uses 384-square letterboxing. There is no scene-dependent lookup, cross-image pooling or adaptation. Historical rounding and quality-99, 4:4:4 JPEG decoding are preserved inside the required RGB PNG output. Masks are L-mode PNGs with labels 0–4. This is the historical single-view v1/v4 path, without a new flip ensemble.

To reproduce historical numerical kernels independently for each image, Restormer384 receives four copies of that image and retains the first output. Stage-specific TF32 settings are reset on every call. In 20 prespecified images, all restoration and segmentation pixels matched the downloaded #19050 results. All outputs also matched after randomizing scene-name prefixes. Peak GPU allocation was 5.60 GiB on an RTX 5080.

The weight archive includes all frozen weights, tokenizer data and license notices. Its URL intentionally awaits owner publication. The requested PyTorch 2.5.1/CUDA 12.4 image builds, but lacks RTX 5080/sm120 kernels. CUDA 12.8 validation is reported separately and is not claimed as validation of the older CUDA image. See reports for exact checker outcomes and unresolved metadata. No public push or upload was performed.
